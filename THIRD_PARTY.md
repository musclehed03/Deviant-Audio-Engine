# Third-party licensing and codec limits

The engine is GPL-3.0-only (see LICENSE). Dependencies retain their licenses.

**Licensing question to review before distribution:** Symphonia 0.5.5 is
MPL-2.0, a file-level copyleft license, not a permissive license. It is used
here as the requested decoder choice for AAC/FLAC/PCM. MPL-2.0's secondary
license provisions generally permit combination with GPLv3; distributors must
verify the exact source notices and fulfill applicable source/notice duties.
Do not describe the complete decoder stack as permissively licensed.

Opus uses `opus-decoder` 0.1.1 (MIT OR Apache-2.0), with the Ogg demuxer `ogg`
0.9.2 (BSD-3-Clause). The decoder's float API internally decodes via signed
16-bit PCM; capabilities report that precision. It is a young implementation;
exercise representative SILK, CELT, and hybrid material before production use.
Only Ogg Opus version 1, mapping family 0 mono/stereo is accepted. Pre-skip,
output gain and final granule trimming are honored; chaining and multiplexing
are rejected. Symphonia AAC profile/container support is limited by that
upstream implementation (AAC-LC in MP4/M4A is covered by our fixture).
Unsupported profiles return errors, never substitute another decoder.

Other direct dependencies: serde and serde_json (MIT OR Apache-2.0), wasapi
(MIT). Cargo.lock pins the full dependency graph. Include upstream license and
notice texts and corresponding source as required when distributing binaries.
Codec patent licensing (including AAC) depends on jurisdiction and distribution;
this repository does not establish patent clearance.

The tiny test fixtures are synthetic 1 kHz sine waves generated by FFmpeg:
`-f lavfi -i sine=frequency=1000:sample_rate=48000:duration=0.05 -ac 2`
with `-c:a flac -sample_fmt s16`, `-c:a aac -b:a 128k`, or
`-c:a libopus -b:a 96k`. They are provided under the repository GPLv3 license;
no third-party recordings are included. FFmpeg is a development-only fixture
generator, never linked or invoked by the engine or CI.
